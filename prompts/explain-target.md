Summarise {{target}} for a reader who is technical but not a penetration
tester.

Cover: what the system appears to be and do, the technologies it runs, where
the meaningful attack surface is, and what the findings mean in practice.

Avoid tool names and jargon where a plain phrase works. Do not speculate beyond
the collected evidence — if something is unknown, name it as a gap.
